## 1. Seasonality (January 2027)

Verified, "cómo dibujar", Spain, 2021–2026:

- Low every summer (Jul–Aug ≈ 25–45), rising from September.
- Yearly high in late Nov / early Dec (7 Dec 2025 = 100, the 5-year peak).
- **Dip in the Christmas/New Year week** (e.g. 5 Jan 2025 = 61, 4 Jan 2026 = 68).
- Strong again from mid-January to March (26 Jan 2025 = 80, 2 Mar 2025 = 91;
  18 Jan 2026 = 80, 1 Mar 2026 = 81).

Conclusion: **no New Year spike.** The shape follows the school year. The
useful window is mid-January to March, so the article should be live and
indexed by early January 2027 (ideally published in December). A
"propósito de Año Nuevo" framing is **not** supported by the data; the
practice plan can start any week instead.

Assumptions not verified: that Mexico follows the same curve (its school
calendar differs, and the data did not load); that the December peak comes
from holiday or school drawing tasks.

## 2. Topic analysis

Autocomplete variations are verified (Spain = ES, Mexico = MX). Intent and
competition are my reading of the results.

1. **Cómo aprender a dibujar desde cero.** Intent: an informational
   roadmap. Autocomplete (ES + MX): "desde cero", "desde cero pdf", "paso a
   paso", "adultos" (ES), "para niños", "con el lado derecho del cerebro"
   (a book).
   - Competitors: course platforms (Crehana, Skillshare, Cursa) and Tuts+.
   - Opportunity: a free, concrete plan that does not funnel to a course.
   - Fit: medium. The app is narrower than "learn to draw anything".
   - Risk: Trends interest is low and noisy (avg 6, many weeks at 0), and the
     page needs new exercise images that don't exist yet.
2. **Apps para aprender a dibujar.** Intent: commercial investigation. ES +
   MX variations: "gratis", "paso a paso gratis", "en papel", "en tablet",
   "en iPad", "para niños", "en digital".
   - Competitors: a vendor list (Clip Studio, "2026"), Guru99, App Store pages.
   - Fit: highest conversion. "En papel" and "paso a paso" match what the app
     does (verify how the team wants to describe it).
   - Risk: an honest comparison means testing competing apps hands-on and
     checking prices on the day. Neither is done, and pricing must not be
     invented.
3. **Cómo dibujar una cara paso a paso.** Intent: a tutorial. Variations:
   "fácil para niños" (the top ES suggestion), "fácil", "de mujer", "de
   perfil", "anime" (ES), "de hombre", "de niña" (MX).
   - Competitors: My Modern Met ES (illustrated), Crehana, Skillshare blog
     and classes.
   - Opportunity: real, verified construction stages and a "common mistake"
     note per step.
   - Fit: very high. The nine Sketch Steps stages are already published in
     the English article.
4. **Ejercicios de dibujo para principiantes.** Intent: a list of
   exercises. The ES autocomplete is dominated by *dibujo técnico*
   (bachillerato, PDF), a different intent. MX shows "para principiantes"
   and "para soltar la mano".
   - Competitors: Crehana, Tuts+ ES, Skillshare.
   - Fit: low to medium. Needs many new images.
5. **Cómo dibujar ojos realistas.** Intent: a detailed tutorial. Mostly
   anime/manga suggestions in both markets; "realistas" is present.
   - Fit: medium. No close-up eye stages exist.
6. **Cómo dibujar una nariz.** Intent: a short tutorial. Suggestions lean
   "fácil", "para niños", "de frente", "realista a lápiz" (ES).
   - Fit: medium. Needs new images.
7. **Cómo dibujar retratos a lápiz.** Mixed intent: "realistas", "de
   famosos", "pinterest" and "cdmx" signal inspiration and commissions, not
   only tutorials.
   - Fit: low as a tutorial keyword.
8. **Cómo dibujar una cara desde una foto.** Intent: a tutorial. Only one
   autocomplete suggestion in both markets: "como dibujar una cara de una
   foto". That is **weak demand evidence**.
   - Competitors: Skillshare classes, Format, My Modern Met.
   - Fit: an exact product match (photo → steps). Best as a supporting
     article.
9. **Método Loomis para principiantes.** Intent: learning the method.
   Variations (ES + MX): "dibujo", "para dibujar rostros", "pdf", "3/4",
   "perfil", "libro", "rostro de frente".
   - Competitors: mostly paid Skillshare classes and a Crehana download.
     Free, illustrated Spanish explanations look thin. **Assumption:** this
     needs checking on the real SERP.
   - Fit: high. The homepage says the portrait tutorials use Loomis.
   - Risk: it overlaps with topic 3, so each article needs a distinct job.
10. **Cómo practicar dibujo todos los días.** Intent: habit and routine.
    "practicar dibujo" variations are scattered: "fácil", "digital", "a
    lápiz", "gestual", "online", "técnico". No "todos los días" phrase
    surfaced.
    - Fit: medium (repeat practice with the app).
    - Seasonal fit: medium, but no New Year spike.

## 4. Is "Cómo aprender a dibujar desde cero" the strongest first topic?

**No. I recommend "Cómo dibujar una cara paso a paso" first:**

- Demand: "como dibujar una cara" has rich autocomplete in both markets, and
  "aprender a dibujar" averages only 6 in Spain.
- Readiness: it can be built only from verified material (the nine stages,
  the reviewed English text) without inventing drawing instructions. "Desde
  cero" needs new exercise illustrations reviewed first.
- Fit: it is the closest match to what the app does.
- hreflang: it is a real translation of the English article, so it is the
  one case where an hreflang link is legitimate (`docs/blog.md` already
  reserves `es: como-dibujar-una-cara`). "Desde cero" must have **no**
  hreflang link to anything.

Risk to clear first: the English article's drawing-accuracy checklist was
never completed (`review.md`: "Not done before publication"). A Spanish
version would inherit any errors. Run that review before translating.

## 5. First article plan

- **SEO title (≈58 characters with the suffix):** "Cómo dibujar una cara paso a paso: método Loomis | Sketch Steps"
  - Alternative: "Cómo dibujar una cara paso a paso (fácil, a lápiz)"
- **Meta description (≈150 characters):** "Dibuja una cara a lápiz en 9 pasos con el método Loomis: círculo, líneas guía, rasgos y sombreado. Con el error más común de cada paso y cómo corregirlo."
- **Slug:** `/es/blog/como-dibujar-una-cara`
- **hreflang:** en ↔ es only, as translations of the same entry.
- **H1:** "Cómo dibujar una cara paso a paso"

Outline. Step content mirrors the reviewed English steps; nothing new is added.

1. Intro (2–3 sentences): what you'll draw, materials, and that Sketch Steps
   generated the nine stages from a portrait photo (keep this disclosure).
2. H2 **Materiales**: the owner-approved list, translated.
3. H2 **El método Loomis en pocas palabras**: what it is, who Andrew Loomis
   was, and the brow-line vs eye-level note.
4. H2 **Los 9 pasos**: H3 per step, each with the image, what/why and an
   "Error común" note:
   - 1 círculo;
   - 2 línea media e inclinación;
   - 3 planos laterales;
   - 4 cejas, nariz y labios;
   - 5 orejas;
   - 6 ojos;
   - 7 nariz y boca;
   - 8 sombreado;
   - 9 acabado.
5. H2 **Plan de práctica de 7 días** (below).
6. H2 **Preguntas frecuentes**: 3–4 answers. Use only questions the text
   answers, e.g. "¿Es fácil para niños?" (the top ES suggestion; answer
   honestly that this guide targets beginners at any age) and "¿Puedo usar
   una foto?".
7. One in-article CTA block (existing `appCta`).

Internal links:

- `/es#demo-title` (the same nine stages);
- `/es` (the app);
- the `/es/blog` index;
- later: the Loomis and "desde cero" articles linking back here.

No link to the English article in the text: the language switcher and
hreflang cover that.

Original images:

- Reuse the nine published stages and the 1200×630 OG card. Rebuild the card
  only if it contains English text.
- Spanish alt text for each stage.
- **No new images until a drawing reviewer has approved them.**
- Optional later: one annotated Loomis diagram (new; needs review).

CTA (from the Spanish homepage voice, no new claims): "¿Quieres pasos como
estos para tu propia foto? Sketch Steps convierte una foto en un tutorial de
dibujo paso a paso." Store badges via `blog_inline`.

Practice plan (built only from the article's own steps, for the reviewer to
check):

- Day 1: steps 1–2, five heads, light lines only.
- Day 2: add step 3; change the tilt each time.
- Day 3: step 4 guidelines; check the spacing with your pencil.
- Day 4: steps 5–7 on yesterday's best head.
- Day 5: a full drawing, steps 1–9.
- Day 6: repeat from a photo of your choice.
- Day 7: compare day 1 and day 6 and redo the weakest step.

It is evergreen: "start any Monday" and no year in the text. That keeps it
valid after 2027 and fits the mid-January to March window.

Spanish editorial checklist (to add as a Spanish addendum to
`docs/editorial/standards.md`, which today only covers American English):

- Neutral Spanish readable in Spain and Mexico: "tú", no vosotros, no
  regionalisms. Prefer "lápiz" and "goma de borrar".
- Loomis glossary in Spanish (línea de cejas, planos laterales, línea media),
  checked by someone who draws.
- Every step matches its image. The English review items are resolved first.
- No volumes, statistics or promised results. Author "Sketch Steps Team".
- Accents and ñ appear in the text and title, never in the slug.
- Native-speaker review: one from Spain, ideally one from Mexico.
- Build checks: the es index becomes indexable; hreflang en/es plus
  x-default; sitemap; one H1; one CTA.
